# Aggregate inputs

The two CSV files contain brand-level pairwise distances used to reproduce the perceptual maps. They contain no respondent identifiers or row-level survey responses.

- `similarity_distances.csv`: holistic brand-similarity distances
- `attribute_distances.csv`: distances derived from structured brand-attribute ratings

The aggregate inputs are included for this analysis and are not a substitute for the underlying survey instrument or respondent-level research data.
